# Commercial Licensing

Isopace is **dual-licensed** by Teqpace Services Ltd.

## The two options

1. **GNU AGPL v3.0 or later** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)) — free and open source.
   Note the key obligation: under **AGPL section 13**, if you run a *modified*
   Isopace and let users interact with it over a network, you must offer those
   users the complete corresponding source of your modified version. In practice,
   most real deployments of a payment switch (custom channels, participants,
   packagers, transaction logic) are "modified" works and trigger this.

2. **Commercial license** from Teqpace Services Ltd. — a paid, proprietary license
   that **exempts you from the AGPL obligations**, so you can:
   - keep your own switch logic, integrations, and configuration confidential;
   - embed Isopace in a closed-source product;
   - run Isopace as a hosted/SaaS service without disclosing source to users.

## Who needs the commercial license?

You likely need it if you cannot or do not want to comply with AGPL section 13 —
typically commercial payment processors, banks, and SaaS providers running a
hosted switch.

You likely do **not** need it if your own project is open source under a
compatible license and you are happy to meet the AGPL obligations.
